Today, I was invited to Agile Leadership Conference 2025 to give a talk on OKR from a leadership perspective.
You can view my slides [here](https://reupke.online/assets/slides/OKRinFuehrungsteams.pdf).

Essential points:
* Start big and begin with the end in mind – people tend to think work-oriented when looking at shorter time frames
* Don't limit yourself by structure or thought patterns people like me would like to impose on your OKR system.
* The fewer objectives, the more impact, focus and efficiency.
* Leaders are responsible to take hard decisions and to enable decision making.
* It takes leadership for OKR to truly work.
